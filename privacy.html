muse-jmb Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Who uses this app

muse-jmb is a personal tool. Its only user is its developer, who authorizes it with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

  • gmail.readonly: after a sign-in attempt, the app searches the developer's inbox only for the verification email sent by Japan Airlines (jmb_confirmation@jal.com) and reads the most recent such messages (at most three) to extract the one-time verification code. It also makes a basic profile request to confirm the authorization is still valid. It does not read any other email.
  • gmail.send: the app sends award-seat notification emails to the developer's own address.

How the data is used and stored

  • The verification code is used immediately to complete the sign-in. It is not saved, logged, or displayed.
  • Email content is not stored by the app. The OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

muse-jmb's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
